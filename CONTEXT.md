# Avatar Animation

The domain concerns people animating their own characters for games, videos, or personal enjoyment.

## Language

**Avatar**:
A person's rigged character, supplied as an FBX file after processing in Mixamo. It may represent their scanned body.
_Avoid_: OBJ, animation

**Try-out avatar**:
A supplied character that lets someone try animating without bringing their own avatar.

**Animation**:
A sequence of character motion, such as a dance, applied to an avatar.
_Avoid_: Avatar

**Pose**:
A static arrangement of an avatar's body, such as a human imitating a T-rex.
_Avoid_: Animation

**Prompt**:
A user's natural-language request describing a desired avatar pose or motion.

**Transition**:
An animation that moves an avatar from a starting pose into a requested pose.

**Loop**:
A repeating animation whose end joins its beginning, such as a walking or dancing cycle.

`root.hover` to the profile-scoped catalog. They author the existing
typed world object-root controls. The shared Blender transaction applies
and bakes the armature object transform while holding the measured local
pose. The world-space measurements check rigid transport of unrelated
targets. Grounded pivot support is limited to one captured sole and an
explicitly raised opposite sole. Hover takeoff releases both captured
soles before moving. Airborne fragments preserve the achieved endpoint.

records the authored bounds, frames, and continuation rules.

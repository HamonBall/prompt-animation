# Stage 05.6c coordinated body movements

Status **complete**, 2026-11-12. Working tree based on `d2f2e74`.
The seven profile-8 builders use the typed fragment and fixed-support bridge.
The current publication contains 48 reviewed cases, 28 compiled fixture
examples and 5,417 motion samples. Final bundle validation and storage
accounting passed.

## body.lower_rise

Both measured floor soles stay fixed through the pelvis descent, hold and
return. Depth defaults to 60 mm and accepts 30–100 mm. Phase and hold default
to 1.2 and 0.2 s, with respective bounds of 0.9–2 and 0.1–0.6 s. Neutral
60 mm and turned crouched 100 mm cases were reviewed on Demo and Mixamo.

## body.step

The stance sole holds while the swing sole releases, clears, and acquires
its translated target. The pelvis advances half the step distance. Side
defaults to left. Distance defaults to 100 mm (60–160 mm), clearance to
60 mm (40–90 mm), and phase to 0.8 s (0.7–1.4 s). Left turned crouched and
right crouched steps were reviewed on both rigs. The exit preserves the
new contact layout.

## body.lunge

Both soles stay planted. The pelvis lowers and advances along measured
heading, then returns. The selected lead sole must be 160–500 mm ahead of
the rear sole. Lead defaults to left and accepts either side. Depth and
advance each default to 60 mm (40–100 mm). Phase and hold use the same bounds
as lower/rise. Demo left lead and Mixamo right lead were reviewed.

## body.takeoff_land

The pelvis compresses and extends with two held soles, then follows the
object root during flight. Both soles settle before the landing compression
and recovery. Defaults are 200 mm flight height (120–350 mm), 75 mm push-off
depth (40–100 mm), 55 mm recovery depth (30–80 mm), and 0.8 s phase
(0.7–1.4 s). Neutral and turned crouched revisions were reviewed on both
rigs after the planted push-off was added.

## body.floor_transfer

Routes are prepared seat (default), standing-to-seated and seated-to-back.
Approach defaults to 2.8 s (2.2–3.5 s). Phase and hold use lower/rise bounds.
The surface defaults to the floor. Prepared contact uses a resolved
horizontal surface. The tracked routes use the floor and keep both soles
planted. Seated-to-back requires calibrated back support and starts from the
solved floor sit. All three routes were reviewed on both rigs.

## body.kneel_rise

Routes are prepared free knee (default) and planted-to-knee. Side defaults
to the first calibrated side. Approach, phase, hold and surface use the
floor-transfer settings. The planted route releases the chosen sole,
raises and folds the free leg, acquires and releases the knee, and
reacquires the original sole. Demo left from neutral and Mixamo right
from turned crouch were reviewed, along with prepared free-knee cases.

## body.crawl

Starts with two measured palms and the lower contacts selected by `support`.
`hands_knees` needs two knees on the floor and a forward-inclined pelvis.
`hands_feet` is the default mode and uses a low stance with two measured soles. Each limb
advances once, keeping three held contacts through its swing. The exit holds
all four contacts. Lead defaults to left. Distance defaults to 60 mm
(40–100 mm), clearance to 40 mm (30–60 mm), and swing to 0.6 s (0.5–0.9 s).
The 100 mm hands-and-feet Mixamo case uses a lower authored entry with its
pelvis 12 mm lower and its wrists 6 mm farther forward.

Hands-and-knees continuation passed two consecutive cycles per rig, starting
from the neutral-heading Demo fixture with left lead and the turned Mixamo
fixture with right lead. The reviewed cases cover 60 mm of travel at 30 mm
clearance and 0.6 s per swing. Both 105-frame views on each rig were inspected.
They show a low torso, alternating palm and knee lift, and trailing feet
clear of the floor. The slow, short-stride cycle starts from prepared
quadruped support. It provides no standing-to-crawl transition.

Maximum measured contact error and floor penetration across the saved
cases were both 0.494 mm. Independent replay and the review hash checks
passed. The post-review storage check measured 3,596,578,699 retained
bytes and zero temporary bytes. The managed report is
`artifacts/maintenance/jobs/stage-05.6c-knee-crawl-review/report.json`.
The earlier hands-and-feet gait remains useful as technical evidence for
that support mode. The user flagged its appearance as stiff.

## Implemented scope and interfaces

All nine required IDs use definition version 1 and the application-owned
`body.torso_pelvis.v1` builder in `src/avatar_app/movement_library/torso_pelvis.py`.
The global catalog has 14 IDs (five existing head/neck plus these nine) and no
automatic rig reviews. The profile-scoped catalog detects available spine chains.

The single-fragment compiler/sampler and Blender action transaction are reused.
There is no family-specific composer/executor, new UI, preset picker, model,
service, dependency installation, or change to prompt interpretation. The public
input remains the prompt. This delivery stops before Stage 05.2.

The full contract is `docs/contracts/spine-pelvis-v1.md`. Generated schemas and
measured, rebuildable default examples are published under
`tests/fixtures/spine_pelvis/v1/`. These are movement-definition v1 records with
calibration-version-3 profiles, not a change to record schema v1.

`with_pelvis_v1(profile)` explicitly extends the existing head-aim profile to
version 3, adding measured `pelvis.position`, `pelvis.orientation`,
`object_root.position`, and `object_root.orientation`. The first pair addresses
the Hips bone; the second measures the actual armature object. Existing profile
versions 1/2 and their controls remain intact. The historical `root.*` controls
and snapshot `root_transform` still alias the calibrated Hips frame; Stage 05.6a
must migrate that naming explicitly before advertising whole-avatar travel.

Torso angles remain in the existing calibrated joint-parent frames. Displacements
follow the captured entry pelvis axes and are stored as absolute world targets;
pelvis orientation is an entry-relative rotation stored as a world quaternion.
The armature object's transform is preserved throughout every fixture.

## Common envelope, ownership and entry/exit behavior

All IDs accept `duration_s` (seconds, default 1, .8..3), `hold_s` (seconds,
default .1, 0..1), and `return_policy` (`return_to_entry`, default, or
`preserve_end`). Timing rounds upward to sampled 30 fps boundaries. Duration is
per transition except circle chords, each a quarter of that duration; the circle
has radial entry/exit and one 32-chord orbit. Holds occur at circle entry, closure,
and recovery, rather than stopping at each chord. Its maximum duration is below
34 seconds, within the unchanged 60-second/1801-sample budget.

The head's world orientation is an explicit owned hold. Arms retain their local
joint pose and follow the torso; they are not advertised as world-fixed hand
targets. Torso-only movements keep pelvis and object root fixed. Pelvis moves
own Hips plus supporting thigh/shin/ankle changes. Spine angular components not
requested remain entry locks. Every solved sample and final measured snapshot
records supporting changes and entry/exit state.

Pelvis moves require both captured floor sole anchors continuously held, with no
acquisition/release ramp. The existing leg/contact solver now accepts an optional
calibrated pole, preserving captured knee planes during turns. Legacy callers
keep the previous default behavior. Reach checks happen before mutation and at
every sampled target; no foot dragging, clamping, standing reset or general
balance simulation is introduced. Identity requests do not re-solve straight
knees. A near-straight pole uses the calibrated parent-relative fallback.

The primary body entry is an explicitly authored 9 cm supported crouch with a
90-degree heading, bent torso/head and asymmetric arm pose. Torso movements also
use neutral entries; lean has an unplanted/free-space fixture. Lowering also has
a turned standing entry. These are test inputs, never executor resets. The
schema ranges are conditional on actual reach and joint limits at the supplied
entry; arbitrary entries are not declared universally feasible.

## Per-ID delivery evidence

All rows inherit the common parameters above. The authoritative full parameter
schemas are `<rig>/schemas.json` in the published fixtures. For every ID, the
default entry/instance/fragment records are `<rig>/<ID>-entry.json`,
`<ID>-instance.json`, `<ID>-fragment.json`. Numeric and visual evidence is under
`artifacts/stages/05.1b/<rig>/<case>/`, on both demo and imported Mixamo rigs.

### torso.lean

Angle default 10°, range −20..20°; plane pitch (default) or roll. Authors the
lowest available calibrated spine segment, with head compensation. Fourteen
cases per rig cover default/small/min/max/zero/preserve, neutral and free-space
entries, both planes/directions, and side-plane boundaries. Example:
`torso.lean.default/`; side examples `torso.lean.roll-min/` and `roll-max/`.
Forward/back and left/right bends preserve pelvis, planted feet and arm joint pose.

### torso.twist

Angle default 10°, range −20..20°; yaw only. Lowest spine segment turns while
pelvis stays fixed and the head keeps its world direction. Seven cases per rig
cover both signs, boundaries, zero, preserved end, and neutral/turned entries.
Example: `torso.twist.default/`. Arm world positions follow the moving torso.

### torso.curl

Angle default 10°, range −20..20° total; pitch only. Distributes equally over
available lower/middle/upper spine segments, with explicit measured return.
Seven cases per rig cover signed curl/unfurl, boundaries, zero, preserved end and
neutral entry. Example: `torso.curl.default/`. Head compensation remains separate.

### torso.wave

Angle default 10°, range −20..20° total; pitch only. One bend/recovery pulse with
8-frame lower-to-middle and middle-to-upper activation offsets. Recovery is part
of the pulse even with `preserve_end`; it does not truncate at the bent peak.
Seven cases per rig cover both signs, extremes, zero, both return choices and
neutral entry. Example: `torso.wave.default/`. Actual measured spine peak order
is checked, not inferred from track metadata. A disposable one-spine demo has a
separate measured and rendered `single_spine_approximation` under
`artifacts/stages/05.1b/single-spine/`; it makes no traveling-wave claim.

### torso.sway

Angle default 10°, range −20..20°; roll (default) or pitch. One positive/negative
excursion, with optional entry return; preserving the end keeps the opposite
excursion. Thirteen cases per rig cover both planes/signs/boundaries, zero,
neutral/turned entries and end behavior. Example: `torso.sway.default/`.

### pelvis.shift

Distance default .01 m, range −.04...04 m; side (default) or forward in the entry
pelvis frame. Twelve cases per rig cover forward/back and lateral shifts, both
planes' boundaries, zero, small amplitude, timings and preserved end. Example:
`pelvis.shift.default/`; forward baseline `pelvis.shift.forward/`. Existing leg
IK preserves both sole anchors and foot orientations while the object root stays fixed.

### pelvis.raise_lower

Distance default −.02 m, range −.04...04 m; entry-pelvis vertical axis. Seven cases
per rig cover lower/rise from crouch, bounds, zero, preserved end and actual
turned-standing lowering. Examples: `pelvis.raise_lower.default/` and
`pelvis.raise_lower.turned-standing/`. An unreachable authored lowering fixture
is rejected before any action/pose/timeline change; numeric parameter violations
also fail through the pure contract validation.

### pelvis.tilt_turn

Angle default 5°, range −10..10°; yaw (default), pitch or roll. Sixteen cases per
rig cover every plane in both directions, each plane's reviewed limits, zero,
small/default amplitude and end choices. Example: `pelvis.tilt_turn.default/`;
other baselines `pelvis.tilt_turn.pitch/` and `roll/`. Hips rotation is distinct
from object-root rotation; planted leg compensation and head compensation are measured.

### pelvis.circle

Radius parameter `distance_m` default .01 m, range −.04...04 m; entry horizontal
plane. A negative radius chooses the opposite starting side, not reverse orbit
direction. One closed 32-chord circle, with radial entry/exit, finishes at captured
entry under either policy. Six cases per rig cover small/default/max/negative
boundary/zero/end policy and timing bounds. Example: `pelvis.circle.default/`.
The measured radial error during the orbit is checked below 1 mm; theoretical
chord error at 4 cm is below .2 mm. Full front/side cycles include support and head locks.

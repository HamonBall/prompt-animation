## Execution

1. Interpret elbow.flex as an upper-arm-relative hinge angle. elbow.orient sets a bend-plane preference around an endpoint; it is not an arbitrary sideways elbow hinge.
2. Separate forearm pronation/supination, wrist flexion/extension, wrist deviation and palm/finger orientation. Use the profile's local frames and optional twist distribution.
3. Support free-space hand.hold with explicit position/orientation frames; reserve actual surface attachment for contact primitives. Trace line/arc/circle paths using shared curve support.
4. Resolve wrist endpoints, palm orientation and elbow poles together. Distinguish hard constraints from soft bend-plane preferences; reject incompatible hard constraints.
5. Provide the target combination 'wrists at chest height, elbows forward' through explicit torso-relative targets and bend-plane controls, without any dedicated prompt handler.

## Testing

- Test palms up/down with straight and bent elbows, wrist flex/deviation after a 90-degree body turn, opposite palm orientations, wrist circles and stationary-wrist elbow-plane changes.
- Measure endpoint/orientation and relative joint angles over time on both rigs. Check the chest-height/forward-elbow combination and a near-straight arm without flips.
- Verify an isolated wrist edit does not change the hand path, root, legs or the other arm except an explicitly permitted supporting adjustment.

3. Keep palm orientation and wrist/arm trajectories independent. Missing fingers yield a precise missing-detail result; a larger recipe may retain the major arm motion with that omission recorded.
4. Register exact and approximate capability status per rig; include semantic measurements for achieved curl/spread.
